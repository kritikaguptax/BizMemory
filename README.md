# BizMemory

A shared, searchable "business memory" web app for small and medium-sized businesses.

Live app: https://biz-memory-flow.base44.app

## Problem

Small businesses depend on knowledge that lives in individual employees' heads: which supplier gives the best credit terms, which customer wants morning delivery, which machine needs regular servicing. This is rarely written down, so when an employee leaves, the knowledge leaves too.

## Solution

BizMemory gives a team one common place to record this knowledge. Anyone on the team can add notes, and anyone can find them later through search.

## Key Features

- Add notes with a category, related name, importance flag and reminder date
- Notes feed with category filters and keyword search
- "Ask Your Business": type a question in plain language (English, Hindi or Hinglish) and get matching notes
- Reminders grouped by Today, Tomorrow, This Week and Upcoming
- Dashboard with a quick summary of notes and contributors
- Multi-business accounts: each business gets a unique Business ID, and its data stays separate from other businesses
- Roles: Owner, Employee and Other, with the Owner approving team members

## Who it is for

General stores, mobile shops, salons, cafes, boutiques, small manufacturing units and other small service businesses.
